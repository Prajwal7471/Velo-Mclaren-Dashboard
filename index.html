    Forecast construction

    Three-scenario fan around FY2025 actuals. Base +30% is ~1.5σ below trailing growth; Bull +45% assumes trajectory holds; Bear +15% assumes regulatory tightening and one major competitor counter-launch. No Monte Carlo — deliberate parametric simplicity for decision use.

    Unit economics

    LTV = monthly contribution ÷ (1 − monthly retention). Standard geometric-series customer lifetime. Inputs are user-adjustable. "Monthly retention" here is a simplification — in practice it should be modelled as monthly churn with cohort decay; this version is built for quick sensitivity.

    Capacity payback

    Payback months = Capex ÷ (Annual incremental capacity × Wholesale price × Gross margin ÷ 12). NPV uses a 10% discount rate over three years at flat run-rate. No terminal value. Assumes the new line sells out — a bullish assumption flagged in the Bear case.
